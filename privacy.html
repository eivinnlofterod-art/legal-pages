Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This personal tool posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What data is used

When an account is connected, the tool receives access tokens and basic profile info (such as user ID, display name and avatar) from TikTok, Google/YouTube or Instagram. This data is used only to upload and publish videos and to check their status.

Storage

Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or analytics.

Deletion

Disconnecting the app in the platform's settings or deleting the local data file removes all stored data.

Contact

For questions, open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
